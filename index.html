What is Liquid Democracy?

Liquid democracy is a participation model that combines the best of two worlds:

  • Direct voting on each initiative
  • Flexible vote delegation
  • Delegation by specific topics
  • Revocability at any time

Instead of choosing fixed representatives, each person decides:

  • When to vote directly
  • Which topics to delegate
  • Who to trust with their vote

Power flows dynamically based on trust and knowledge.
